Account & Data Deletion

Delete your Tefsen account

You can request deletion of your Tefsen account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how to request deletion, what data is deleted, what data may be retained, and how long the process may take.

Request account deletion

How to request account deletion

Send an email to support@tefsen.com with the subject line:

Tefsen Account Deletion Request

Please include the email address or username connected to your Tefsen account so we can identify the account.

Data we delete

  • Account profile information
  • Name, username, and email address
  • Profile photo, if uploaded
  • School or university profile details
  • Posts, questions, comments, and answers where deletion is technically possible
  • Verification-related profile information

Data we may retain

  • Records required for security, fraud prevention, or legal compliance
  • Payment or subscription records handled by Google Play, where required
  • Moderation logs needed to keep the community safe
  • Anonymous or aggregated data that no longer identifies you

Deletion timeline

We aim to process account deletion requests within 30 days. Some limited records may be retained for a longer period where required by law, security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or payment processing requirements.

Subscription note

If you purchased a Tefsen subscription through Google Play, deleting your Tefsen account does not automatically cancel your Google Play subscription. You should cancel the subscription from your Google Play account if you no longer want to be charged.

Contact

For account deletion or privacy questions, contact us at support@tefsen.com.
