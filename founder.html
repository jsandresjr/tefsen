The journey

Building technology for learning, connection, and creativity.

Js Andres Jr is the Founder of Tefsen and a Software & Web Developer. He develops the Tefsen software and web platform, bringing product development, web technology, and education technology together in one project.

Tefsen is being built as a social education network designed to help students discover knowledge, share ideas, join meaningful discussions, and grow through connected learning.

Alongside his technology work, he is an independent music artist and digital creator. His broader work combines software development, web development, science, music, and visual creativity.

Founder principles

What guides the product.

01

Students are contributors.

A learning community becomes more valuable when people can share what they know, ask better questions, and add useful perspectives.

02

Technology should feel human.

Tefsen aims to make educational technology feel approachable, social, clear, and genuinely useful in everyday student life.

03

Creativity expands possibility.

Experience across software, web development, music, and digital creation informs a product direction where technology, expression, and learning can work together.
