The journey

Science, creativity, and technology in one direction.

Js Andres Jr is a Physical Science student, artist, and digital creator whose work spans creative platforms including Adobe Stock and Zedge.

As founder of the Tefsen app, he is building a social education network designed to help students discover knowledge, share ideas, join meaningful discussions, and grow through connected learning.

His work brings together analytical thinking from science, visual creativity from digital art, and product building through education technology.

Founder principles

What guides the product.

01

Students are contributors.

A learning community becomes more valuable when people can share what they know, ask better questions, and add useful perspectives.

02

Technology should feel human.

Tefsen aims to make educational technology feel approachable, social, clear, and genuinely useful in everyday student life.

03

Creativity expands possibility.

Experience as an artist and digital creator informs a product direction where design, expression, and learning can work together.
